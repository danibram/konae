Request an invitation

Making apps on this site is by invitation while it is small. Leave your email and whoever runs the site sends you a code. You use it once, to create your account.

  • Your email is used to send the code and for nothing else.
  • You will also need an AI key from Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter. The assistant runs on your own account with them.
  • Rather not wait? Konae is open source, and you can run it on your own server with Docker.
