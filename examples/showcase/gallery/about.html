About

Lena Varga photographs the edges of places: harbours at low tide, tram depots after midnight, fields the week before harvest. She works on medium-format film and scans every frame herself.

Prints are made to order on cotton rag paper in three sizes. Commissions for editorial and architectural work are open for the coming season.
